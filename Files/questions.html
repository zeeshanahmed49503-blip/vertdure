Balanced fertilization with a lawn treatment will result in a dense, vigorous, and healthy lawn. This will help the lawn better resist harmful organisms (diseases, insects, and weeds).

explaination

Like any plant, grass needs nutrients, which it obtains from the soil and through fertilizers applied as part of a maintenance program. An unfertilized lawn becomes more vulnerable to insects, diseases, and weeds. By applying fertilizers in small doses, spread throughout the season, you allow your lawn to receive the necessary nutrients at the right time, without harming the environment.
